About Us

Tholusal is a proudly South African, majority black women-owned (51%) Level 1 BBBEE Building & Maintenance company. We provide high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective construction, renovations, plumbing, and general building maintenance solutions.

Our foundation is built on meticulous planning of safe building solutions and a deep commitment to high-quality workmanship, ensuring every project, from routine maintenance to full-scale renovations, is executed to the highest quality standard. We are committed to delivering exceptional quality, on-time project completion, and client satisfaction.

Run by professionals with extensive experience across a variety of industries, including real estate, construction, engineering, and governance amongst other skills, we bring a unique blend of technical expertise, strategic oversight, and hands-on operational excellence.
