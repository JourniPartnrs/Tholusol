Specialist Interior Renovation: Modern Bathroom Upgrade

Complete overhaul of an outdated bathroom, including new plumbing, tiling, waterproofing, and fixture installation for enhanced durability and style.

Project Snapshot

Client Sector: Residential Maintenance
Location: Gauteng Region
Service: Full Bathroom Renovation & Plumbing Update
Duration: 3 Weeks
Focus: Advanced Waterproofing & Compliance

1. The Challenge: Outdated and Failing Systems

The client's primary bathroom was suffering from outdated fixtures, poor tiling, and an aging plumbing system prone to leaks. The goal was a complete interior renovation to modernise the space, improve functionality, and—critically—replace the internal pipework to ensure long-term reliability and prevent future water damage, all while maintaining the tight project timeframe.


2. Tholusol’s Strategy & Execution

A. Comprehensive Plumbing Replacement

Our work began with the demolition of old tiling and plaster to expose the existing systems. We installed new copper and PVC piping for both supply and waste lines, embedding them securely within the wall structure. This proactive replacement of aging infrastructure prevents costly emergency repairs down the line.

B. Expert Tiling and Waterproofing

Specialist services included applying a multi-layer waterproofing membrane to the shower area and floor before tiling commenced. We installed modern, durable ceramic tiles on the walls and floors, ensuring proper grading for drainage and a clean, contemporary finish.

The strategic replacement of all copper and PVC pipework embedded in the walls ensures the longevity and reliability of the new bathroom.
