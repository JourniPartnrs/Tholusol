Turnkey Construction of Multi-Unit Rental Complex

Comprehensive project management delivers income-generating assets on time and on budget in the Gauteng Region.

Project Snapshot

Client Sector: Property Investment
Location: Gauteng Region
Service: Full Turnkey Construction
Duration: 6 Months
Compliance: OHS Compliant (Safety), B-BBEE Level 1

1. The Client's Vision & Challenge

The client, a property investor, required the swift and compliant development of a multi-unit complex designed for high-occupancy rental income. The critical challenge was achieving a balance between **cost-effective construction** and **high-quality, low-maintenance finishes** (essential for minimizing future operating costs) within a **tight, investor-mandated timeline** of six months to ensure rapid ROI.

2. Tholusal’s Strategy & Execution

A. End-to-End Project Management

Tholusal provided complete design-to-handover construction services, overseeing every aspect from foundation pouring, structural erection, utility installations (plumbing and electrical), and full external and internal finishes. This single-point management approach eliminated delays and streamlined communication.

B. Engineered for Longevity and Safety

Given the high-use nature of rental property, our strategy included specialized material selection. We utilized durable waterproofing techniques and applied heavy-duty timber sealants to the external balconies and stairs to withstand weather and constant use, minimizing the client’s long-term maintenance burden. Site adherence to the **OHS Act** was rigorously enforced.
